PRIVACY POLICY — REXRUN: LEVEL LAB

Package ID: com.studio.rexrun  |  Effective Date: October 27, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to REXRUN: LEVEL LAB ("the App"), a 2.5D side-scrolling dinosaur arcade runner and level creator for Android devices. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through strict adherence to Google Play Developer Program Policies, the Google Play Families Policy, COPPA, and GDPR guidelines.

The App does not require user registration, login accounts, or personal profile creation, and we do not operate external servers that store your personal data.

2. Information We Collect & Local On-Device Storage

Zero Personal Information Collected by Developer: We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, physical address, photos, contacts, microphone audio, camera feed, or precise geographic location.

100% Local Game Progress Storage: All gameplay state is saved strictly locally on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences under the key REXRUN_SAVE_DATA_V1:

  • Unlocked campaign stages (Stages 1–100), star ratings, and Endless Mode high scores.
  • Collected in-game coins and unlocked Baby T-Rex cosmetic skins.
  • Custom courses created in the Level Lab Editor and local Ghost Replay time-trial recordings.
  • Audio volume sliders (SFX and Music), Haptic Vibration preference, Persistent Dust Trails toggle, and Reduced Motion accessibility settings.

3. Level Lab Share Codes (REX-v1)

The Level Lab Editor allows players to export and import custom obstacle courses using deterministic text strings prefixed with REX-v1-. These codes contain only compact obstacle grid coordinates and level metadata (stage title, world theme, and creator nickname). Share codes are generated offline on your device and are only shared if you manually copy and paste them.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) (com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads) solely to offer optional Rewarded Interstitial ads at natural end-of-run breaks.

  • Strict Opt-In & Non-Intrusive Flow: Ads never appear during active gameplay. Before any Rewarded Interstitial ad is shown, a clear introduction screen explains the in-game coin reward (+35 Bonus Coins) and lets you decline and continue playing immediately without watching an ad.
  • Family-Safe & Non-Personalized Ad Configuration: Every ad request is programmatically restricted to General Audience ratings (MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G), tagged for child-directed treatment (TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE), tagged for users under the age of consent (TAG_FOR_UNDER_AGE_OF_CONSENT_TRUE), and configured for non-personalized ads only (npa=1).
  • No Advertising ID Tracking: The com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission is explicitly removed from the application manifest so the Android Advertising ID (AAID) is not accessed for behavioral profiling.
  • SDK Diagnostic Data: Google AdMob may automatically process basic device and network information (such as IP address, coarse country/region, device model, screen dimensions, and ad impression/completion events) strictly for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and aggregate ad delivery in accordance with Google's Privacy & Terms.

5. Android Permissions Used

  • android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used exclusively to load and display optional Google AdMob Rewarded Interstitial ads when an internet connection is available. The entire game works offline without internet access.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used for subtle tactile haptic feedback on jumps, coin pickups, and hazard collisions. You can disable Haptic Vibration Feedback at any time in the in-app Settings menu.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

REXRUN: LEVEL LAB is designed to be safe and enjoyable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). All ad requests enforce MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G, child-directed protection flags, and non-personalized serving.

7. User Data Deletion & Control Rights

You have complete control over all data stored on your device at any time:

  • In-App One-Tap Reset: Open Settings & Data Safety from the Title Screen and tap Reset Data → Confirm Permanent Reset to immediately and permanently erase all local save progress, custom Level Lab courses, Ghost Replays, and unlocked skins.
  • System-Level Deletion: You can also clear the App's storage via your Android device's Settings → Apps → REXRUN: LEVEL LAB → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the App.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new gameplay features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be bundled directly inside the App and published on our hosted Privacy Policy page.
